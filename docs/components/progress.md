# Progress Bar

Progress Bar menampilkan progress determinate ketika application mengetahui nilai current, minimum, dan maximum.

v0.3.5 hanya menyediakan determinate Progress Bar.

## Purpose

Gunakan Progress Bar ketika progress dapat diukur.

Contoh:

```text
Upload 65%
Import 40%
20 of 30 records processed
3 of 5 steps completed
